02 // SELECTED WORK
CASE STUDY 01 // MOTIVE

Engine Immobilizer

Empowering fleet managers to remotely immobilize hijacked vehicles in high-risk territories.

ROLEDesign Lead
IMPACT+$7M ARR Expansion
TIMELINE2-Month Sprint

The Challenge

When Motive started expanding into the Mexican logistics market in Q3 2023, local sales feedback was definitive: 90% of prospective clients demanded remote engine immobilization. We had lost several major enterprise deals to local competitors over this single missing capability. Remote immobilization immediately transformed from a future roadmap item into a high-stakes blocker between Motive and a massive national market. We had a strict two-month window to design and ship the full interface.

The Constraints

The engineering landscape was highly fragmented. The administrative management interface was in the middle of a massive rewrite from 1.0 to 2.0, meaning the core Vehicles detail list page we needed to redesign was completely unbuilt. Concurrently, our hardware department was sourcing third-party device modules for the first time, introducing complex latency constraints on how cellular command packages were dispatched and confirmed.

Field Insights & Synthesis

I interviewed fleet operations managers on-site to understand high-stress safety workflows. The resulting designs addressed critical real-world realities: authorization had to stay strictly with operations dispatchers (never drivers), and signal-jamming attempts required automated hardware alerts. We prototyped and tested five UI configurations. A persistent, highly colored contextual banner won usability tests—immobilization is rare (less than 1% of fleets at any time), so the status required extreme emphasis during emergencies without cluttering daily telemetry views.

The Deliverables & Output

We designed and shipped a high-fidelity real-time command banner, live map track-and-follow modules, automated incident timeline loggers that pair dashcam footage with vehicle coordinates for audit reports, and cellular tamper alerts. The product launched in Q1 2024, fully unlocking the Mexican logistics market and driving a seven-figure ARR expansion within the first quarter.

CASE STUDY 02 // EDUCATIVE

Workflows

A unified, real-time multiplayer workspace built for engineering team coordination and document execution.

ROLEHybrid Manager & IC
IMPACTClosed $100K Deal
TIMELINE8-Month Initiative

The Challenge

As the internal product organization at Educative scaled from 50 to 600 people, collaborative speed broke down. Product specifications, design specs, and engineering roadmaps were scattered across isolated document silos. Teams suffered from severe context-switching fatigue, leading to missed sprints and communication gridlock. We set out to design "Workflows"—a central, multiplayer project workspace built to optimize organizational outcomes.

My Role

Leveraging my tenure at the company, I collaborated directly with our executive sales, product, and engineering heads. I designed the core product architecture as an individual contributor while managing our growing design division, aligning designers and brand illustrators with our engineering execution.

User Research & Architecture

I conducted structural interviews with 31 internal and external participants (18 Engineering Managers and 13 Software Engineers) to isolate operational bottlenecks. EMs struggled to track real-time approval pipelines, sign-off requests frequently sat stale for days in Slack backlogs, and technical implementation discussions became completely lost in detached threads. We designed Workflows around a multiplayer document editor that contextually embeds task management boards, structured approval routing loops, and a centralized workspace overview.

The Outcome

Workflows successfully eliminated status-chasing communication loops across our development divisions. The product served as our key competitive differentiator during subsequent sales cycles, directly securing a $100K enterprise sales contract.
